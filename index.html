How a welded pipe is made

Rolled. Welded. Polished to a shine.

  1. StripStainless coil is slit into a strip about as wide as the pipe’s circumference. For a 22 mm OD pipe that is roughly π × 22, or 69 mm.
  2. FormingRolls bend the strip a little at each stand until the two long edges meet.
  3. WeldingThe edges are fused into one seam along the full length, glowing white-hot behind the torch.
  4. FinishingThe seam is ground flush, the surface is polished to a mirror, and the pipe is sized and cut to length.

    The right grade for where it lives.

    Both are austenitic stainless steels. The difference is mostly nickel. 304 carries more of it and resists corrosion better. 202 swaps part of the nickel for manganese and costs less.
